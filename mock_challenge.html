Color Transportation

Carry the blocks to their designated positions.

🚧 Mission Specification

Challenge: Starting in the green square, move to carry the blue block into the blue square, then the red block into the red square, and green block into the green square.

🎯 Mission Parameters

  • Starting Position: Green square
  • Success Criteria: Use arm motors and robot movement to carry all the blocks into their designated color. End with the robot inside the green square.

💡 Navigation Strategy

Measure how much the robot needs to move to reach the blocks and to make it into each of the squares.

🤖 Programming Approach

🌊 Movement Sequence Planning

Step 1: Measure the distance the robot needs to move to reach the first block.
Step 2: Have the robot open and close the arm on the blocks to carry it.
Step 3: Measure the distance to the designated square.
Step 4: Measure the degrees the robot needs to turn for reaching the next square.
Step 5: Repeat this for the other 2 blocks.
Step 6: Use AI by specifying each step in detail.
Student's Example Command:

"Move my robot by x inches, then open its arm by x degrees counterclockwise, then move forward 1 more inch, and close arm by x degrees clockwise, last move the robot by x inches. When this is done, have it turn by x degrees..."

🛡️ Safety Protocol

  • ⚠️ Remember to use gyro turns, not normal turns, for more precise movement.
  • 🔄 Multiple attempts encouraged
  • 📏 Measure or guestimate the degrees needed to turn and inches needed to for movement.
  • 🤝 Team collaboration recommended

Advanced Color Transportation

Surrounded by crates, use your compass to turn, scan them to identify crate colors, and collect the one that matches your orders

🚧 Mission Specification

Challenge: Starting from the green square, move to carry the colors placed on the numbers (red on 5, blue on 1, green on 3) to their matching color rectangles before returning back to green.

🎯 Mission Parameters

  • Starting Position: Green square
  • Success Criteria: Bring each color to their rectangles and end at the green square.

💡 Navigation Strategy

Measure the distances for each step the robot needs to carry out.

🤖 Programming Approach

🌊 Movement Sequence Planning

Step 1: Measure the degree the robot would need to reach the first block.
Step 2: Have the robot reset the yaw before performing any movement. Have it turn towards the block and pick it up using the arms.
Step 3: Measure the degrees the robot needs to turn to start heading towards the first block's rectangle.
Step 4: Measure the distance it needs to make it to that rectangle.
Step 5: Repeat this for all the blocks.
Step 6: Tell the AI each step, including a request to reset the yaw, before sending the prompt.
Student's Example Command:

"Have my robot move forward x inches, move its arm by x degrees counterclockwise, then move forward 1 more inch, and close the arm by x degrees clockwise. Then have it rotate x degrees counterclockwise, and move forward x inches. Open the arm again by x degrees clockwise..."

🛡️ Safety Protocol

  • ⚠️ Remember to use gyro turns, not normal turns, for more precise movement.
  • 🔄 Multiple attempts encouraged
  • 📏 Measure or guestimate the degrees of turns and inches of movement
  • 🤝 Team collaboration recommended
