Robopalooza Challenge Prep

For the next few weeks, you will be practicing for Robopalooza. You will be given three weekly challenges that closely resemble the official Robopalooza challenges. During this time, instructors will not assist with connecting the robot and will only be allowed to provide hints to help complete each challenge. Once a challenge is completed, students will move on to the next challenge. Good luck and have fun!

Week 2: Robopalooza Challenge Prep: Movement

Robopalooza Challenge Prep: Movement Challenge

The Challenge: Each team must navigate the robot through the course, following the black line from start to finish. This will be a timed challenge where speed and accuracy are essential. The team with the fastest time while successfully completing the course wins Start and End Area: Green Start Area
Goal: Precision and Speed is key! Make sure at least one wheel remains on top of the white surface at all times while navigating the course. Line Follow is not required!

Robopalooza Challenge Prep: Line Follow Challenge

The Challenge: Each team will be given 30 minutes to line follow around the entire course as fast as possible. After the 30 minutes are over each team will attempt the course. The fastest team to complete the course wins.
